title: Multi-brand token system
category: Design systems
description: I turned a white-label product that was slow to customize into a token system in Figma. Launching a new brand now means switching a variable collection, not rebuilding a file.
cover: ./tokens_cover.webp
client: Eccocar
date: June 2024 - August 2024
context: Eccocar was a **startup** that launched, operated and scaled digital mobility services, working with car rental companies, dealerships and fleet managers around the world. Its product was sold as **white label**, so every client needed to see it with their own identity.
challenge: Adapting the product to each new client was **slow and hard to scale**. Every brand required setting up a new file with its own colors, typography and logo, and design improvements **didn't carry over** easily to the other versions.
role: As **UX Designer**, I designed and built the token system with Figma variables, defined its naming and structure based on Material Design, created adaptable components and worked with the front-end developer to keep design and implementation in sync.
solution: A **token system in Figma** based on **Material Design 3**, with one variable collection per brand plus light and dark themes. Components are built once and change identity when you switch collections.
results:
  - metric: "3 levels"
    description: of tokens (**settings**, **ref** and **sys**) that separate base values, each brand's identity and semantic usage.
  - metric: "300+"
    description: variables organized for color, typography and spacing.
  - metric: "2 themes"
    description: light and dark for every brand, validated on real components and flows.
  - metric: "0"
    description: colors entered by hand. Palettes are imported from a JSON file generated by Material Theme Builder.

Creating a new brand became a fast, standard process. Each brand's logo is added to the asset library.
  - title: Implementation and validation
    media:
      type: video
      src: /projects/tokens/tokens_multimedia_4.mp4
    description: I applied the new tokens to the existing components and flows and checked that switching themes (light and dark) and brands worked across all of them, validating the system **before handing it off to development**.
learnings:
  - A token system lets a white-label product **scale** without multiplying design work.
  - '**Naming** and **documentation** matter as much as the variables: they are what lets others use the system.'
  - A solid design system speeds up the business and improves **collaboration between design and engineering**.
